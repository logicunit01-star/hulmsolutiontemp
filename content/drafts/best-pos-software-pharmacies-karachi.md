Pharmacies and medical stores in Karachi operate in a fast-paced environment with strict inventory control requirements. Unlike standard retail, a medical dispensary handles thousands of active medicine SKUs, strict batch tracking, near-expiry drug write-offs, doctor prescription records, and wholesale distributor procurement.

## Complete Outline

### 1. Essential Challenges in Karachi Pharmacy Management
- **High SKU Volumes:** A typical Karachi medical store stocks between 4,000 and 15,000 pharmaceutical items across generic and brand names.
- **Batch and Expiry Sensitivity:** Selling expired medication carries severe health hazards and regulatory penalties under drug regulatory authorities.
- **Distributor Credit & Reorders:** Pharmacy procurement involves multiple distributor credit accounts requiring detailed vendor ledger tracking.
- **Counter Speed:** Peak dispensary hours in Karachi (near hospitals and residential zones) require sub-second barcode and product search to avoid long patient queues.

### 2. Critical Features Needed in Pharmacy POS
1. **Batch & Expiry Date Tracking:**
   - Ability to associate batch numbers and expiry months with every incoming medicine shipment.
   - First-Expiry, First-Out (FEFO) dispensing logic during counter checkout.
2. **Low-Stock and Near-Expiry Alerts:**
   - Automated reports highlighting medicines reaching expiry within 30, 60, or 90 days for return to distributors.
   - Minimum threshold notifications for critical emergency drugs.
3. **Manufacturer & Distributor Management:**
   - Linked supplier records showing pending invoices, returns, and payment schedules.
4. **Fast Medicine Search:**
   - Instant search by brand name, generic salt, or barcode.
5. **Customer Ledger (Udhaar Khata):**
   - Managing credit accounts for regular neighborhood families with automated balance statements.

### 3. Desktop Software vs. Cloud Pharmacy POS in Karachi
- **Legacy Desktop Software:** Susceptible to hard drive crashes, data corruption during Karachi power cuts, and lack of remote visibility for owners managing multiple pharmacy counters.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I import my existing pharmacy medicine list into the POS?
Yes. Compatible pharmaceutical product data (product name, barcode, purchase price, selling price, and supplier) can be imported from Excel spreadsheets. Hulm POS provides guided assistance for spreadsheet catalogue migrations.

### How does expiry management work at the counter?
When dispensing medications with multiple batches in stock, the cashier selects the batch closest to expiry (FEFO), ensuring older batches are cleared before newer deliveries are opened.

### Does the POS require constant internet to function?
Cloud POS systems require an active internet connection (via broadband, fiber, or mobile 4G hotspot). Data is saved directly to cloud infrastructure, preventing local data loss during counter machine failure.
