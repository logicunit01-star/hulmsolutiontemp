# How to Declare Multiple Branches and POS Terminals on FBR's Portal

Expanding a retail brand across multiple shopping districts—such as opening branches across Gulberg, DHA, and Model Town in Lahore—requires strict adherence to the Federal Board of Revenue's (FBR) Tier-1 integration guidelines. Under federal sales tax regulations, retailers must register every physical business premise and each individual POS checkout terminal with the tax authority.

## Complete Outline

### 1. Regulatory Context for Multi-Branch Retailers
- Under FBR Sales Tax Rules (Chapter XIV-AA), any retailer qualifying as a Tier-1 retailer due to chain operations (two or more outlets) must integrate all billing points.
- Issuing receipts from an undeclared terminal or registering multiple stores under a single counter ID violates FBR fiscalization guidelines.

### 2. Pre-Requisites for FBR Terminal Registration
- Active National Tax Number (NTN) and Sales Tax Registration Number (STRN).
- Registered Sales Tax Business activity on FBR IRIS.
- Commercial tenancy agreement or ownership documentation for each branch location.
- Certified FBR-integrated POS software with verified API connection credentials.

### 3. Step-by-Step Portal Declaration Process
- **Step 1: Declare Business Premises in IRIS:**
  - Log in to the FBR IRIS portal using administrative credentials.
  - Access Registration Modification Form 181.
  - Add each new branch address, utility consumer numbers, and designated business name.
- **Step 2: Generate POS Terminal Credentials:**
  - Access the FBR POS integration gateway (e.fbr.gov.pk).
  - Register individual POS terminals corresponding to physical checkout desks at each branch.
  - Obtain unique POS Terminal IDs and security encryption keys.
- **Step 3: Configure Cloud POS Multi-Branch Profiles:**
  - Input terminal keys into your cloud POS terminal settings.
  - Verify test invoice generation and QR code readability before launching commercial sales.

### 4. Operational Best Practices Across Branches
- Centralized inventory transfers between central warehouses and retail counters.
- Cashier shift logs linked to specific registered terminal numbers.
- Automated daily closing reconciliations between physical cash drawers and digital FBR submissions.
