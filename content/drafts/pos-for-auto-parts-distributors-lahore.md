# POS for Auto Spare Parts Distributors in Lahore: Parts Compatibility and Khata Ledgers

Badami Bagh in Lahore is the undisputed epicenter of Pakistan's automotive aftermarket, supplying engine parts, suspension assemblies, transmission kits, brake components, and lubricants to auto mechanics, fleet operators, and retail dealers nationwide. With fast daily wholesale transactions and thousands of imported replacement parts, managing inventory by hand or through disconnected paper registers leads to costly errors.

## Complete Outline

### 1. The Challenge of Automotive Part Numbers & Fitment
- Customers often request parts by car make, model year, and engine code rather than the official manufacturer part number.
- High-efficiency automotive POS workflows require:
  - Vehicle fitment indexing (e.g., searching "Corolla 2018 front brake pads" displays all compatible Japanese, genuine OEM, and aftermarket brands).
  - Cross-reference part interchange search (interchanging OEM numbers with aftermarket manufacturer codes).

### 2. Digitizing Mechanic & Fleet Credit (Khata) Ledgers
- Over 60% of daily transactions in wholesale auto markets involve credit (Khata) extended to trusted workshop mechanics and fleet managers.
- Eliminating disputed balances and missing receipts:
  - Assigning credit limits and payment grace periods per workshop.
  - Recording partial cash settlements and updating ledger balances instantly.
  - Generating digital balance statement summaries shareable via WhatsApp or SMS.

### 3. Multi-Warehouse (Godown) Inventory Visibility
- Auto parts businesses frequently maintain a sales front counter in Badami Bagh while bulk inventory is stored in two or three nearby godowns.
- Real-time multi-location inventory tracking ensures counter salesmen never turn away buyers when stock is available in storage godown 2.
- Inter-warehouse transfer slips tracking stock movement and shrinkage.

### 4. Bulk Price Tiers: Retail Walk-In vs. Wholesale Mechanic Rates
- Automated multi-tier price lists:
  - Walk-in retail customer pricing.
  - Workshop mechanic trade discount pricing.
  - Bulk distributor volume rates.
- Preventing counter salesmen from selling below minimum acceptable profit margins.

### 5. FBR Tier-1 Invoicing for High-Turnover Auto Traders
- Automated FBR invoice generation with printed QR codes on wholesale bills.
- Recording buyer NTN/STRN on commercial sales invoices to ensure input tax adjustments for registered corporate buyers.
