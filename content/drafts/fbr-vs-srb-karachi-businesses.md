# FBR vs SRB: What Karachi Businesses Must Know

Operating a commercial business in Karachi means navigating two distinct tax jurisdictions: the **Federal Board of Revenue (FBR)**, which oversees federal sales tax on goods, income tax, and Tier-1 retailer integration, and the **Sindh Revenue Board (SRB)**, which administers the Sindh Sales Tax on Services.

## Complete Outline

### 1. The Core Distinction: Goods vs. Services
- **Federal Board of Revenue (FBR):**
  - Jurisdiction: Federal (all across Pakistan).
  - Scope: Sales tax on tangible goods (retail stores, electronics shops, supermarkets, clothing boutiques, pharmacies).
  - Digital Mandate: Tier-1 retailer integration under Sales Tax Rules. Real-time invoice transmission and FBR QR codes.
- **Sindh Revenue Board (SRB):**
  - Jurisdiction: Provincial (Sindh / Karachi).
  - Scope: Sindh Sales Tax (SST) on taxable services under the Sindh Sales Tax on Services Act 2011.
  - Affected Sectors: Restaurants, cafes, caterers, hotels, event planners, IT services, salons, and maintenance services operating within Karachi and Sindh.

### 2. Retail Requirements Under FBR in Karachi
- **Tier-1 Retailer Criteria:**
  - Operating as part of a national or international retail chain.
  - Operating in an air-conditioned shopping mall, center, or plaza.
  - Shops with cumulative electricity bills exceeding statutory thresholds.
  - Wholesale-cum-retail outlets.
- **Invoicing Requirements:**
  - Mandatory fiscal invoice numbering.
  - FBR POS invoice monitoring QR code and FBR invoice number printed on customer receipts.
  - Real-time API reporting to FBR servers.

### 3. Restaurant and Hospitality Requirements Under SRB
- Restaurants and cafes in Karachi are subject to Sindh Sales Tax on Services administered by SRB.
- Reduced tax rates may apply for card/digital payments versus cash payments as per prevailing Sindh budget notifications.
- Invoicing must display the taxpayer's National Tax Number (NTN) and SRB Registration Number (SNTN).
- Karachi food businesses often require split configurations when selling both packaged FMCG goods (federal) and dine-in catering services (provincial).

2. Ensure receipt templates support custom NTN, STRN, and SNTN prints.
3. Validate that tax line items calculate accurately per category.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does a restaurant in Karachi register with FBR or SRB?
Restaurants in Karachi primarily register with the Sindh Revenue Board (SRB) for Sindh Sales Tax on food and catering services. However, corporate income tax and employee withholding tax remain under the jurisdiction of the FBR.

### What is printed on an FBR-integrated receipt?
An FBR-integrated POS receipt displays an official FBR invoice number, customer verification QR code, store STRN/NTN, itemized price breakdown, and applicable sales tax amount.
