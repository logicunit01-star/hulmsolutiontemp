# Hulm local website SEO and UX audit — 1 October 2026

## Executive result

The local React/Next.js website preserves the current production URL set, titles and meta descriptions while presenting a clearer POS-first journey. The core idea is consistent: start with Hulm POS, then add connected operational modules as the business grows.

The audited production build generated 106 routes successfully. The 44-page non-blog SEO scan passed in full, the homepage passed 71/71 checks, and the priority Features, Pricing, Industries and FBR suites passed 181/181 combined checks.

## Journey review

1. **Homepage — healthy.** Clear Pakistan POS positioning, visible product evidence, FBR relevance, industry paths, pricing context, reviews and two conversion routes.
2. **Features — healthy.** The page explains billing, stock, staff and connected modules without replacing the POS-first message.
3. **Pricing — healthy.** Plan progression and add-on context are clear, with direct links to relevant feature and compliance pages.
4. **Industries — healthy.** The index and priority industry pages create useful topical clusters and retain their established URLs.
5. **FBR — healthy, subject to business/legal verification.** Search intent, visible FAQs and schema align. Compliance claims should always be reviewed when FBR requirements or the product implementation changes.
6. **Demo/contact — healthy visually.** The forms are clear and the CTAs are consistent. Successful delivery into the production lead destination still requires an end-to-end test after production environment variables are configured.

Fresh desktop and mobile evidence is stored in `planning/local-audit-2026-10-01/`.

## SEO preservation evidence

- Existing production paths remain available with trailing-slash canonicals.
- All 58 stored production pages retain the production title and meta description; a direct live/local comparison also returned no differences during the audit.
- Legacy `/insights/`, `/blog/` and `/case-studies/` paths now reach their replacements in one permanent redirect.
- The 60-page internal-link crawl reports 0 broken links, 0 redirecting links, 0 links missing a trailing slash and 0 generic anchor labels.
- Page and post sitemaps retain every live URL. The only additions are `/apps/` and `/book-a-demo/`.
- Page copy and structured data are present in server-rendered HTML, so conventional search crawlers and text-based answer engines can access them without running client JavaScript.
- Organization, WebSite, WebPage/Breadcrumb and relevant SoftwareApplication, Service, Article, ItemList and FAQ entities are emitted where appropriate. Blog authors now resolve to a stable editorial-team entity.
- Robots rules allow the major search and answer-engine crawlers while keeping API routes out of the index.

## Corrections made during this audit

- Removed 101 redirect-producing internal link patterns from rendered blog content and added an automated check to prevent recurrence.
- Removed the remaining author redirect and nine non-canonical internal links.
- Reduced legacy redirect chains to one hop.
- Replaced the obsolete homepage test with the approved current homepage specification.
- Rewrote unsupported or overly promotional statements on Inventory, Order Management and Reporting pages.
- Clarified About and Contact positioning without changing their production titles or descriptions.
- Added a stable editorial-team structured-data entity and connected article authorship to it.
- Replaced two failed remote About-page images with verified local assets.

## Remaining launch controls

- Rankings cannot be guaranteed by any migration. Preserve DNS/hosting stability, do not change the verified URL map at launch, and monitor Google Search Console coverage, crawl errors, impressions and clicks daily during the first week and weekly for at least a month.
- Submit both sitemaps after launch and inspect the highest-traffic URLs manually in Search Console.
- Validate Analytics/Tag Manager events, form delivery, WhatsApp links and the thank-you flow in the production environment before announcing the site.
- The current lint run has 0 errors and 27 warnings, mainly legacy `<img>` performance recommendations. These are a measured Core Web Vitals optimization backlog, not URL/indexing failures.
- `/terms-and-conditions/` has no contextual body in-link but remains linked globally in the footer. `/about/` has two contextual body in-links plus global navigation. This is acceptable, though future editorial content can strengthen both naturally.
