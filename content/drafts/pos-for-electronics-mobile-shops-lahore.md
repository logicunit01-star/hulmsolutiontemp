# POS for Electronics and Mobile Shops in Lahore: Serial Number and Warranty Tracking

Operating a consumer electronics store, mobile phone shop, or computer hardware dealership in commercial tech hubs like Hafeez Centre (Gulberg) or Hall Road requires operational controls distinct from standard retail. High ticket values, individual serial numbers (IMEI/ESN), varying supplier warranties, and customer trade-ins demand rigorous tracking at checkout.

Modern [POS in Lahore](https://hulmsolutions.com/pos-software-lahore/) ensures that every serialized device sold is permanently indexed for post-sales support and warranty verification.

---

## Complete Outline

### 1. Serial Number and IMEI Lifecycle Tracking
- Standard retail items are tracked by quantity; electronics must be tracked by unique serial identifiers.
- Capturing serial/IMEI numbers during goods receiving (purchase orders) and verifying them at sales checkout.
- Instant invoice search by serial number: resolving customer warranty claims in seconds without searching paper archives.

### 2. Warranty Management & Expired Coverage Prevention
- Capturing dual warranty timelines:
  - Official brand warranty (e.g., 1-year official manufacturer warranty).
  - Store testing warranty (e.g., 7-day checking warranty for used devices).
- Printing serial numbers, warranty terms, and testing expiration dates directly on the sales receipt.

### 3. Customer Trade-Ins and Second-Hand Device Procurement
- Recording pre-owned device trade-ins against new purchases:
  - Documenting seller CNIC number, phone number, and physical device condition for legal compliance.
  - Adding refurbished or pre-owned inventory with adjusted cost valuation.

### 4. Technician Repair Tracking & Job Cards
- Integrated repair ticketing for mobile and laptop service centers:
  - Assigning repair job cards with customer complaints and estimated repair costs.
  - Tracking replacement spare parts (screens, batteries, charging ports) consumed from shop inventory.

### 5. Cashier Controls and Counter Shift Audits
- Preventing price tampering on high-value gadgets through role-based cashier permissions.
- Tracking split payments (cash + card + bank transfer) to ensure drawer cash matches physical daily counts.
