# FBR vs PRA: Sales Tax Obligations for Lahore Retailers and Restaurants

Operating a commercial trade or restaurant in Lahore requires navigating two distinct fiscal frameworks: the **Federal Board of Revenue (FBR)**, which oversees federal sales tax on retail goods and Tier-1 e-invoicing integration, and the **Punjab Revenue Authority (PRA)**, which governs Punjab Sales Tax on Services.

Selecting the right [POS software in Lahore](https://hulmsolutions.com/pos-software-lahore/) requires understanding which regulatory authority governs your invoices and ensuring your billing counter prints compliant invoice identifiers.

## Complete Outline

### 1. The Core Distinction: Federal Goods vs. Provincial Services
- **Federal Board of Revenue (FBR):**
  - Jurisdiction: Federal (all across Pakistan).
  - Scope: Sales tax on tangible physical products (retail shops, fashion pret boutiques, auto spare parts, consumer electronics, supermarkets, pharmacies).
  - Mandate: Tier-1 retailer integration with real-time digital invoice generation, unique FBR invoice numbers, and verifiable QR codes.
- **Punjab Revenue Authority (PRA):**
  - Jurisdiction: Provincial (Punjab / Lahore).
  - Scope: Punjab Sales Tax (PST) on services governed by the Punjab Sales Tax on Services Act 2012.
  - Affected Sectors: Dine-in restaurants, cafes, catering services, beauty salons, event management, and technical services operating across Lahore.

### 2. Retail Requirements Under FBR in Lahore
- **Tier-1 Retailer Criteria:**
  - Retailers operating in air-conditioned shopping malls and commercial plazas (e.g., Packages Mall, Emporium Mall, Mall of Lahore).
  - Multi-outlet retail chains operating across multiple commercial sectors.
  - Retailers whose cumulative annual electricity bills exceed statutory thresholds.
  - Large-scale wholesale-cum-retail traders.
- **Receipt Printing Requirements:**
  - Unique FBR fiscal transaction number printed on every invoice.
  - Verifiable QR code linking directly to FBR transaction records.
  - Real-time API transmission from POS billing terminal to FBR PRAL gateway.

### 3. Restaurant and Hospitality Rules Under PRA
- **Punjab Sales Tax on Services:**
  - Food prepared and served in restaurants, cafes, bakeries with seating, and fast-food outlets in Lahore is classified as a taxable service under PRA jurisdiction.
  - Differentiated sales tax rates depending on payment method (standard rate on cash vs. concessionary rates on digital/card payments where applicable under Punjab budget notifications).
  - Compulsory display of Punjab National Tax Number (PNTN) on customer food bills.

### 4. Multi-Branch Operations Across Lahore
- Businesses managing outlets in multiple Lahore zones (e.g., Gulberg, DHA, MM Alam Road, and Model Town) must maintain location-specific POS terminal tracking.
- Every individual terminal must be declared on FBR IRIS and synchronized with the central cloud inventory database.
